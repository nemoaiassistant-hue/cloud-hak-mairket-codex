Fair questions

The things we'd ask too.

Isn't this just SEO?

They're cousins, not twins. SEO gets you found when someone types into Google. AI visibility gets you recommended when someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI for a shortlist. The signals overlap (reviews, clear info, your Google profile) but AIs also read things Google mostly ignores. We handle both sides — and our monthly report shows exactly what changed.

How fast will I see results?

The fixes land in 7–10 days. When AIs start mentioning you depends on them — they refresh on their own schedule, typically within weeks. That's exactly why we do monthly re-checks instead of promising miracles: you watch the trend, month by month, with screenshots. No spin.

My website is fine. Do I still need this?

A good website helps, but AIs don't read sites like humans do. They cross-check your site, your Google profile, your reviews, and dozens of directories. If any of those disagree or are thin, you drop out of the shortlist. The free check shows you exactly where you stand — then you decide.

What do you actually change?

On your site: clear service and area pages, plain answers to real customer questions, and technical files that tell AIs who you are and what you do. Off your site: your Google Business Profile tidied and aligned, review signals strengthened, and stale directory listings cleaned up. Nothing gimmicky — AIs punish gimmicks.

Someone else manages my website. Can I still use this?

Absolutely — plenty of our customers have their own web developer. Each month you get the report plus any updates written as a simple document your developer can paste straight in: exact text, where it goes, why it matters. You just forward it. No access needed, nobody's toes stepped on.

Is the free check really free?

Yes. It's a day of our time and we do it because half the people who see their screenshots want the fix immediately. The other half say "interesting" and walk away — fine by us. (It's a sales tactic, we're just honest about it.)
